# 🎓 DBU Connect

### Connecting Students. Sharing Ideas. Empowering Campus Commerce.

**DBU Connect** is a social media and e-commerce platform designed for the Debre Berhan University (DBU) community. It provides a digital space where students can connect, communicate, share ideas, discover products, and buy and sell items within the university community.

The goal is to make campus life more connected, collaborative, and convenient through one platform.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🌐 Social Media
- Create and share posts with the university community.
- Interact with other students and their content.
- Share ideas, announcements, and campus experiences.
- Build connections within the DBU community.

### 🛍️ E-Commerce Marketplace
- Buy and sell products within the university community.
- Discover products and services offered by other students.
- Publish product listings with descriptions, prices, and images.
- Contact sellers to discuss products and purchases.

### 👥 User Accounts
- Register and log in to the platform.
- Manage personal profiles.
- Connect and interact with other university community members.

### 🔐 Security and Administration
- Secure user authentication.
- Manage users and platform content.
- Support a safer and more trusted campus marketplace.
